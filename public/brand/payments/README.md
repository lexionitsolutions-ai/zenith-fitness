# Payment brand assets

Original assets downloaded from the brands' public websites. Display unchanged,
preserving proportions and colors, on a white background with clear space.

- Google Pay: https://www.gstatic.com/instantbuy/svg/light_gpay.svg
  Guidelines: https://developers.google.com/pay/api/web/guides/brand-guidelines
- PhonePe: https://www.phonepe.com/press/_astro/phonepe-logo.DIxulQvf.svg
  Source: https://www.phonepe.com/press/
- Paytm: https://pwebassets.paytm.com/commonwebassets/paytmweb/header/images/logo.svg

These assets identify payment options; they do not indicate a gateway partnership
or automatic confirmation of payment. Regenerate with
`python scripts/download-payment-logos.py` when the source assets change.
